Everything You Need

Complete leave lifecycle management for employees and administrators

📝

Apply for Leave

Employees can submit leave requests at any time with date ranges, leave types, and optional comments.

📊

Balance Tracking

Employees always know how many leave days remain per leave type — no guesswork, no HR back-and-forth.

✅

Approval Workflow

Administrators review, approve, or reject requests with remarks. Employees get instant status updates.

⚡

Bulk Allocation

Admins can allocate leave days to all employees of a type at once — perfect for annual leave resets.

🎛️

Admin Dashboard

A central view showing pending requests, total approvals, and rejected entries across the entire org.

🔐

Role-Based Access

Powered by ASP.NET Core Identity with Administrator and Employee roles — secure by design.
